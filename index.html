Common Questions

$0 down lease purchase, answered.

Straight answers to what CDL drivers ask most about leasing to own a truck with Valor Logistics.

What is a $0 down lease purchase truck?

A $0 down lease purchase lets a CDL driver start driving a semi truck with no money down. Each weekly payment goes toward owning the truck, and the title transfers to you at the end of the term.

Do I need my own truck to join Valor Logistics?

No. Valor Logistics provides the truck. You lease it to own with $0 down and drive as an owner-operator.

How much are the weekly payments?

Payments are a fixed weekly amount disclosed up front for each available truck, with no hidden fees. You always know your exact payment and how many weeks remain until the truck is yours.

What CDL do I need for the program?

A valid Class A CDL is required to lease-purchase a semi truck through Valor Logistics.

Will I own the truck at the end of the lease?

Yes. Valor Logistics is a true lease-to-own program — when your payments are complete, the truck is yours.

How do I get started?

Apply online in a few minutes. We review your info, walk you through the program, match you with an available truck, and get you rolling — no pressure and no obligation.

Can I lease purchase a truck with bad credit or no credit?

Our lease purchase program is built for working CDL drivers, not credit scores. We focus on your ability to drive and earn, so many drivers who can't get approved at a bank or dealership can still lease to own a truck with Valor Logistics.

How is a lease purchase different from being a company driver?

As a company driver you drive someone else's truck and never own anything. With a lease purchase you run as an owner-operator, keep more of what you earn, and every payment builds equity toward owning your semi truck outright.

What kind of trucks can I lease to own?

We offer well-maintained Class 8 semi trucks — sleepers and day cabs from the major makes. You can see every available unit, its specs, and its exact weekly payment on our Available Trucks page before you apply.

How long is the lease purchase term?

Each truck has a set number of fixed weekly payments disclosed up front. When you complete those payments the truck is paid off and the title is yours — no balloon payment and no surprises.

What happens if my truck breaks down?

We keep our trucks road-ready and work with you on maintenance so a breakdown doesn't end your week. Every available unit lists its condition up front, and our team is a phone call away when you need support.
