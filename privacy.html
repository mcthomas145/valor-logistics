Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Version 1.0

Valor Logistics ("Valor," "we," "us," or "our") operates a $0-down lease-purchase program for CDL-A drivers. This Privacy Notice explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to our website at haulvalor.com and to our lead forms, including Instant Forms on Facebook and Instagram (together, the "Services").

1. Who We Are

Valor Logistics is an Indiana business. For privacy questions, contact us at info@haulvalor.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact and identification information: name, phone number, and email address, provided when you submit a lead form or contact us.
  • Application and qualification information: CDL class (e.g., CDL-A), years of CDL-A driving experience, the type of equipment you are interested in (dry van, reefer, open deck), and when you could start.
  • Technical and usage data: information generated automatically when you use the Services, such as device and browser information, collected through our website or ad platforms.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Contact you about our $0-down lease-purchase program and available driving opportunities;
  • Evaluate your fit for the program and match you with appropriate equipment;
  • Improve our recruiting and advertising.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. How We Share Information

We share personal information only as needed to operate our business and as described below:

  • Advertising and lead-management providers. We use lead-ad services (including Meta, for lead ads on Facebook and Instagram) and share with them only what they need to perform that function.
  • Service providers. We use providers that help us contact and onboard drivers and operate our website, under appropriate contractual protections. We share only what each provider needs to perform its function.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Valor Logistics, our drivers, or others.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Notice.

5. How We Protect Information

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption of data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to address security events promptly.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to consider your application and for legitimate business purposes, or to meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, update, or delete it, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise a right, or to ask us to stop contacting you, contact us at info@haulvalor.com or (317) 516-0653. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses only the cookies and local browser storage necessary to operate. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on our site. (Ad platforms where our ads run, such as Meta, are governed by their own policies.)

9. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for business use and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Location

Our infrastructure and service providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they maintain facilities. We rely on providers that maintain recognized security and privacy safeguards.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. We encourage you to review this Notice periodically.
